Privacy & cookies

Updated 20 October 2026. GamePulse is an independent gaming news index operated by Eraldo Musella.

How the site works

GamePulse links to publishers’ websites. Visiting an external article or using Google Translate takes you to another service with its own privacy policy. Hosting is provided by Render, which processes connection data, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure this site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Local storage and offline use

Your chosen language is saved in your browser as gamepulse-language until you clear it. If your browser supports it, a service worker caches the app shell, icons and previously fetched news for offline access. Clear this site’s browser data to remove these preferences and cached content.
